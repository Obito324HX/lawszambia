Teaching a kinder community

Rescuing animals is only part of the work. LAWS also welcomes children and communities to the shelter to learn how to care for animals.

A school visit at the shelter

In August, learners from Trackside Learning Studio spent a morning at the LAWS shelter, meeting the animals and learning how the team cares for them.

Children are always welcome at the shelter. They come to learn about animals and how to treat them, and the team says it learns just as much from them: their curiosity, their questions and the way they see the world.

The learners also raised a K500 donation towards the shelter's work, which helps LAWS keep caring for animals in need.

From the LAWS Facebook page, 13 August 2026.
