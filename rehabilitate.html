Time to heal

Healing takes time and care. At the shelter, every animal gets what they need to recover and thrive.

  1. Medical attention

    Injuries, illness and neglect are treated as soon as an animal arrives.

  2. Proper nourishment

    Regular, good food helps animals rebuild their strength.

  3. Emotional support

    Many of our animals have been hurt by people. Patient, gentle handling helps them trust again.

  4. Ready for what comes next

    When an animal is healthy and confident, they are ready to meet a new family.
