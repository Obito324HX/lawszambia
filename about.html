About LAWS

The Lusaka Animal Welfare Society rescues, rehabilitates and rehomes abandoned and neglected animals in Lusaka, Zambia.

Our mission

To save abandoned pets and find them loving homes.

We rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned and neglected animals. Every animal deserves a second chance at life, with the care, love and support they need to recover.

We work with our community and with passionate volunteers to build a future where every animal has a safe and loving home.
